What Kiiri does

Not another chat window.
A friend on your screen.

Most AI tools want you to come to them. Kiiri sits where you already are, notices what's happening, and helps when you ask.

01 · Agents

Know what your agents are up to without switching windows.

Kiiri hooks into Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and Antigravity. While they work, Kiiri gets busy. When one finishes or needs you, Kiiri tells you.

  • One-click hooks, no config files to edit
  • Ding and a bubble when an agent needs your input
  • Several sessions at once, each with its own state
02 · Your screen

Ask about anything on your screen. Kiiri looks, answers, and points.

Hold CtrlAlt and talk, or type your question. Kiiri reads the screen, streams the answer into a bubble, and points to the exact spot.

  • Push-to-talk, typed questions, or drag a box around a region
  • Spoken answers with natural voices, or your system voice offline
  • Drop the cat on something to summarize, explain, translate or solve it
03 · Your character

Make it yours. Then make a few more.

Name it, give it a personality, repaint it in the pixel editor, or bring in community pets in the open petdex format. Run several at once. Each one keeps its own memory.

  • Follows your cursor with its eyes and leans toward you
  • Pick it up by the scruff and it dangles, then settles where you drop it
  • Remembers its work, so it can bring things up later
04 · Research & reports

Say what you need. Get a sourced PDF back.

"Research the EV market in Europe and make me a PDF report." Kiiri searches the web, shows you the plan and the sources first, and writes the file only after you approve.

  • Talk or type: click the mic, or give a command
  • Web research with the pages it used listed in the report
  • PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, saved locally with one-click undo
05 · Hands on your computer Plus

Ask it to do the clicking. It asks before anything risky.

"Open Safari and find tomorrow's weather in Istanbul." Kiiri plans the steps, you approve, and it clicks and types for you while you watch.

  • You approve the plan before it touches anything
  • Stops and asks before sending, buying, deleting or typing a password
  • Press Esc or Stop at any moment; a strip shows every step

For developers

Your agents can drive it, too.

Kiiri ships a local MCP server. Any agent can make it talk, point at the screen, react, or ask it what's on screen. Everything runs locally and needs a token. Secrets and file paths are stripped before anything appears in a bubble.

kiri_say show a bubble and optionally speak it
kiri_point point at a spot on the screen
kiri_react working, thinking, done, needs-you
kiri_ask_screen ask about the screen and get text + coordinates
kiri_status what Kiiri is doing right now
~/.claude.json 
// Kiiri adds this for you when agent monitoring is on.
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "kiri": {
      "command": "…/Kiiri/hooks/kiri-hook-runner.sh",
      "args": ["kiri-mcp-server.js"]
    }
  }
}

// Then just ask your agent:
"When the tests finish, have Kiiri point at the first failure."
Privacy

Where does your screen go?

Kiiri only looks at your screen when you ask it to. You choose where that picture goes.

Local nowhere

Your screen→Ollama on your machine

Nothing leaves your computer. Works on a plane with Wi-Fi off.

Your own key direct

Your screen→Anthropic / OpenAI / any compatible API

Kiiri talks straight to your provider with your key. We never see it. Prefer keys off your laptop? Put them on your own proxy.

Kiiri Cloud not stored

Your screen→Kiiri proxy (in memory)→model

The free plan (25 asks a month), Plus and Pro. Screenshots pass through memory and are never written to disk or used for training.

No telemetry by default. The whole client is open source, so you can check for yourself.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is Kiiri really free?

Yes. The desktop app is open source under the MIT license, and everything that runs on your machine is free with no time limit. Bring an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI or any compatible provider, or run models locally with Ollama. You also get 25 AI asks a month on Kiiri Cloud with just your email. Plus and Pro add more of what costs us money to run: hosted AI, web research, computer use, voices and sync.

Is it always watching my screen?

No. Kiiri only captures your screen when you ask: push-to-talk, a typed question, a region you select, or an automation you set up yourself. The cat's eyes following your cursor happens locally and doesn't involve any screenshots.

Which AI models does it use?

Your choice. Claude, GPT and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint work with your own key. Local models work through Ollama. Kiiri Cloud picks a good default for you: Claude Haiku on the free plan, Claude Opus on Plus and Pro.

How does it work with Claude Code, Cursor and Codex?

Turn on agent monitoring and Kiiri installs small hooks for the agents you use, so it can react when they start working, finish, or need you. It also registers its MCP server, so the agent can make Kiiri talk, point, or look at your screen.

Can I host the AI part myself?

Yes. kiiri-proxy is a small open-source Cloudflare Worker you deploy to your own account in about two minutes. Your API keys stay there, each device gets its own token, and chat, screen questions and voice all go through it. It's the same proxy core that Kiiri Cloud runs on.

Does it slow my computer down?

Kiiri sits idle in a small transparent window. AI only runs when you ask for something, and the animations pause when nothing is happening.

macOS says the app can't be opened. What do I do?

Signed and notarized builds are on the way. Until then, right-click the app and choose Open the first time. On Windows, choose More info → Run anyway.

Can I make my own character?

Yes. Repaint a character in the built-in pixel editor, or drop a pet in the open petdex format (a pet.json plus one sprite sheet) into ~/.kiri/pets and it shows up in the menu.

Where does the name come from?

Kiri (霧) means mist in Japanese, and (桐) is the paulownia tree, which is where the little leaf on Kiiri's head comes from. The double ii is its face. Say it "kee-ree".
