Everything you need to write about Kiiri. Please don't stretch, recolor or redraw the logo or the cat.

In one sentence

Kiiri is an open-source desktop companion: a little cat that lives on your screen, keeps you company, reacts to your coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) and, when you ask, looks at your screen and points at the answer. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux, and can run fully locally.

Name

Always written Kiiri, with a capital K and two i's, and pronounced "kee-ree". The double ii is the cat's face: two eyes with little dot eyebrows. In the logo, the two dots are terracotta.
